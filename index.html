⚙️ What is CoreCage?

CoreCage manages background tasks on your computer. When you play games, background programs use resources that your game needs. CoreCage identifies these programs and limits their activity. This process frees up processor power. Your games run smoother with higher frame rates as a result.

The application uses specific performance data to verify efficiency. It changes nothing deep within your Windows system that could harm your computer. CoreCage works alongside standard anti-cheat software.
